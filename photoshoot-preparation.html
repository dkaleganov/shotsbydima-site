What to Wear & How to Prepare for Your Headshot


Your session is built to capture confidence and approachability —your digital handshake. I guide micro‑expressions, posture, and angles so you look like yourself on your best day. We review images together as we go, refine what’s working, and zero in on the winners.

Headshots (Chest‑Up)


Tight framing that prioritizes expression and eye contact. Expect guided coaching, clean backgrounds, and multiple outfit options.

Typical timing: ~45–90 minutes for individuals. For team days, plan ~15-30 minutes per person.

Personal Branding / Portraits


A broader mix of images—headshots, ¾, and portraits—for websites, PR, and social. We vary backgrounds, lighting, and distance to tell a cohesive story.

Typical timing: 2–3 hours, depending on looks.

The Flow (Day‑Of)


  1. Plan– We confirm your goals and desires, outfits, and backgrounds.
  2. Shoot– I direct expressions and posture; we capture a range of options.
  3. Review– We evaluate images on screen during the session and keep only the strongest.
  4. Select– Choose your finals on set or from a curated proof gallery.
  5. Retouch & Deliver – Professional retouching on selected images; delivery sized for web and print (typical turnaround 3–5 business days; rush available).

What to Bring (Over‑Pack = Options)


  • Tops: Bring more than you think —if it’s not here, we can’t shoot it. Aim for solids and rich textures over busy patterns.
  • Layers:Jackets, blazers, cardigans, or light outerwear give instant variety.
  • Necklines:Choose shapes that flatter your jawline and neckline (collar, V‑neck, crew).
  • Fit:Tailored beats loose. Steam/iron ahead of time; bring a lint roller.
  • Accessories:Keep jewelry minimal and non‑distracting unless it’s part of your brand.
  • Glasses:Bring all pairs you use; we’ll manage reflections on set.
  • Color tips:Mid‑tones and deeper neutrals photograph beautifully. If you go bold, bring a neutral backup.


Wardrobe Guidance by Use Case


Executives / Corporate:

  • Men/masc: jacket/shirt (tie optional); bring 2–3 shirt options.
  • Women/femme: blouse/dress/jacket combos; bring 2–3 top options with different necklines.


Personal Branding:

  • Think in “looks” (smart‑casual, elevated, and one statement look).


Actors/Performers:

  • Range wins. Pack layers across types you play; avoid logos; bring texture.

Hair, Makeup & Grooming


  • Makeup (optional; paid option available):A camera‑friendly, natural base works best. We can build intensity as we go. Bring lip balm, pressed powder/blotting papers, and your preferred lipstick/gloss.
  • Hair:Arrive with hair styled as you’d like to be seen. Avoid last‑minute drastic changes. Bring a brush/comb and any products you rely on.
  • Facial Hair:Decide on clean‑shaven vs. scruff.
  • Skin:Hydrate, sleep well, and avoid new treatments or heavy sun exposure 48–72 hours before your shoot. Minor blemishes/stray hairs are handled in retouching.

Friendly Note


If you’re unsure about an outfit or styling choice, bring it. Options give us creative flexibility, and we’ll decide together on set.
