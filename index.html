Photography FAQs

Where does Shots by Dima photograph clients?

Sessions are photographed on location across San Jose and the San Francisco Bay Area, including offices, event venues, homes, and other agreed spaces in Santa Clara, Cupertino, Sunnyvale, Mountain View, Palo Alto, Menlo Park, Redwood City, and San Mateo.

What photography services does Shots by Dima offer?

Executive headshots, LinkedIn headshots, personal-branding portraits, creative portraits, team and corporate headshot days, and on-site editorial black-and-white portrait stations for events.

How long does a headshot session take?

Individual sessions typically run 45–90 minutes. For team headshot days, plan roughly 10–15 minutes per person with a mini studio built on-site.

When are finished photos delivered?

Professionally retouched final images are normally delivered in 3–5 business days. Rush turnaround is available when needed.

Can Shots by Dima photograph an entire company team?

Yes. Team headshot days can match existing brand guidelines, backgrounds, crops, lighting, and delivery specifications so every employee's portrait looks consistent.
