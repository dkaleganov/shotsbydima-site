Executive headshots, made for the Bay Area

Shots by Dima is a premium executive headshot photographer based in San Jose, serving founders, executives, and professionals across the San Francisco Bay Area. Whether you need a polished LinkedIn headshot, press and speaking portraits, or images for your company's org charts and portals, every session is built to capture confidence and approachability—the digital handshake that shapes a first impression before a word is spoken.

Sessions take place at your office, event venue, home, or another agreed location across San Jose, Santa Clara, Cupertino, Sunnyvale, Mountain View, Palo Alto, Menlo Park, Redwood City, San Mateo, and the wider Bay Area. We bring professional lighting and background options, then coach wardrobe, expression, and posing so you look your best on cue.

For teams, we run corporate headshot days that match your brand guidelines and delivery specs, keeping every portrait consistent across your website and internal tools. Individual sessions are professionally retouched and delivered in 3–5 business days, with rush turnaround available when you're on a deadline.

Frequently asked questions

Where do sessions take place?

At your office, event venue, home, or another agreed location across the Bay Area, including San Jose, Santa Clara, Cupertino, Sunnyvale, Mountain View, Palo Alto, Menlo Park, Redwood City, San Mateo, and beyond.

How long does an executive headshot session take?

Individual sessions run about 45–90 minutes. For teams, we plan roughly 10–15 minutes per person and build a mini studio on‑site.

When will I get my photos?

Professionally retouched, polished finals are delivered in 3–5 business days. Rush turnaround is available when you're on a deadline.

What should I wear?

We'll plan looks together before the shoot. Solid, well‑fitted clothing in colors that suit your brand photographs best — and we coach wardrobe and expression so you look confident and approachable.

Do you photograph whole teams?

Yes. We run team days that match your brand guidelines and delivery specs, so every headshot looks consistent across your website, org charts, and portals.

Can you match an existing set of company headshots?

Absolutely. We can match background, crop, and lighting to your current standards so new hires blend seamlessly with the rest of the team.
