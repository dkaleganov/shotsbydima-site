Premium Portrait Station

A premium, on-site portrait studio for corporate and major life events when quality and attention to detail truly matter. Flattering direction, refined lighting, timeless style of black-and-white portraits on crisp white background, and instant shareables your guests will actually keep.

Not a booth. A professional studio—at your event.

Corporate


Pro‑grade portraits your team will love. From summits to offsites, we build a mini studio that scales—polished, consistent portraits with fast delivery and branding options for attendees and execs. Ideal for corporate events, leadership meetings, and conferences across the Bay Area. Friendly coaching for confident expressions.

Events


Whether it's galas, fundraisers, brand launches, weddings, or something else, give every guest a red‑carpet moment. A modern, editorial portrait experience—timeless black‑and‑white—guided by a pro photographer. Guests step in, get directed, and leave with portraits worthy of a frame. For weddings and milestone celebrations, guest may order additional luxury prints, wall art or photo books.

How it works

Step In


A bright, professional set with a welcoming team

Get Directed


Friendly and flattering posing and expression coaching, highlighting your best side

Polish & deliver


Tethered capture for instant review, retouching, and on‑site black‑and‑white finishing; fast digital delivery; optional immediate prints.

After the event


Curated gallery; optional ordering of prints, wall-arts, photo books from a professional photo laboratory straight from the gallery

What’s Included


  • Photographer‑led sessions
  • Studio lighting + pure white backdrop
  • Tethered capture and on‑site B&W finishing
  • Fast digital delivery

Add‑Ons


  • On‑site prints (4×6)
  • Fine retouching bundle post‑event
  • Various formats prints, wall-art, post-cards, or photobooks ordering via the gallery
